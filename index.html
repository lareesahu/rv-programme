The brief

Who is on the stage

In his own words, from the introduction he shared in Hangzhou.

Robert Vicencio has spent more than thirty years in entertainment, across musical theatre, film and television. He was on the original creative team of PHANTACITY 《幻乐之城》, China's most expensive reality show of 2018 for Hunan TV, as Format Creator, Consultant Director and Guest Director. He directed Faye Wong 《王菲》 and Sarah Brightman, and creatively supervised 48 individual shows across 12 episodes.

He played General Thuy in Miss Saigon for more than 3,000 performances in ten cities, from Sydney to London's West End. He then starred as one of the 12 Apostles in Andrew Lloyd Webber and Universal's Jesus Christ Superstar, and in Filmax's science fiction feature Arachid.

He has directed and worked with Bvlgari, Lincoln, Piaget, Chivas, Atlantis, Absolut, Nokia, New Balance and the Disney Princess Concerts. He composed and co-wrote Singapore's Sports National Anthem Live Our Dreams, wrote children's musicals and his own musical Rock, Scissors, Paper, and published The IX Invaluables to Auditioning in Chinese and English.

As an art lover he founded and curates My Other Brain, The Alternative Art Exhibition, now in its sixth year. As a serial entrepreneur he imports and distributes the vermut from his family's bodega in Méntrida, Spain, and runs his own bar and brand, Vermuthology. He now works on AI and robotics, and has just closed his second TEDx talk on how ChatGPT and AI will disrupt the entertainment industry.

VI The Next

Now teaching machines to tell stories

The same instinct, a different stage: what happens when the crew is made of models.

Two TEDx talks: Back to the Future in 2017, and TEDxSuzhou in 2023 on how ChatGPT and AI will disrupt the entertainment industry.

In between, the work moved from directing people to directing systems: AI animated series, robotic performance, and the question of what a stage looks like when the cast can be generated. He also still casts shows, as Casting Director for The Han Show and as Casting Consultant across China, because someone has to know which face belongs in which room.
